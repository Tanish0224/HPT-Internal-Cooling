# Main design decisions

Each entry gives the decision, the reason, and its status. The numbers (ED-035 and so on) are the identifiers used in
the project records. The numbering is not contiguous: decisions without a public entry are omitted. Status labels are explained in [`README.md`](README.md).

| ID | Decision | Reason | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| ED-035 | Start a new blade lineage (second design) from the velocity triangles; keep the initial design unchanged | The initial design's outer shape was wrong (thickness/chord about 0.31–0.33, wedge leading edge) and its passages formed no flow circuit; fixing either needed the other re-derived | DECISION |
| ED-036 | Keep the assumed operating point and anchor it to the NASA/GE Energy Efficient Engine HPT; coolant 879 K / 2.95 MPa at the root; metal limits 1,357 K local, 1,226 K bulk | The reference gives a public set of consistent temperatures, pressures and coolant fractions | ASSUMED, anchored to a source |
| ED-037 | Rotor exit swirl −20° | Hub reaction about 0.2 (0.196, hub still accelerating) and mean reaction 0.32 against 0.34 in the reference; tested between −15° and −30° and kept | DECISION |
| ED-038 | Airfoil from a camber line plus a √ξ thickness law, so the leading-edge radius is real | An earlier airfoil definition produced a wedge; the nose radius is now measured with a shrinking-window curvature fit and converges to the specified 1.8 mm (checked on the section definitions; not repeated on the final CAD) | DECISION |
| ED-039 | 66 blades; hub axial chord 35 mm; loading shaped by a panel-method optimisation | Diffusion ratio is insensitive to blade count between 66 and 72, so the smaller count minimises cooled surface; a 31 mm hub chord gave diffusion 1.27 and a pressure-side hump | CALCULATED (model output, not published); checked by 2-D RANS |
| ED-040 | Thickness and nose radius set by the cooling packaging, then checked aerodynamically | These are the smallest values that hold six cavities with 1.0 mm walls and 0.9 mm webs; RANS loss Y = 0.043 at mean | DECISION |
| ED-041 | Two root-fed circuits; trailing edge fed from the first, coolest pass of a three-pass serpentine | A two-pass serpentine with large passages warmed the coolant only 60–80 K; three smaller passes raised it to about 970 K at about 3 % flow; the reference engine feeds the trailing edge from the first pass | DECISION, quantified by the model |
| ED-042 | Film rows only where the model shows they pay: two suction-side rows, no pressure-side row | A pressure-side row from B1 over-cooled the pressure side and drained B1, leaving the suction side over B1 hot | CALCULATED |
| ED-043 | External boundary conditions from 2-D RANS | Adiabatic-wall temperature and heat-transfer coefficient from two runs per section; an integral boundary-layer model is the cross-check. A defect was found and fixed: the solver's default energy equation left out viscous dissipation, pressure work and kinetic energy, so the adiabatic wall recovered only about 30 % of the dynamic temperature; all runs were repeated | SIMULATION (earlier geometry) |
| ED-044 | Keep a single leading-edge impingement row although its jet Reynolds number (about 42,000) is above the correlation range | The leading edge is not the limiting location: a 30 % lower internal coefficient there changes peak metal temperature by 1 K | Stated limitation with sensitivity |
| ED-045 | Minimum film-hole diameter 0.45 mm | The unconstrained optimiser went to 0.35 mm; the reference used 0.51 mm and notes that small holes cost more, plug and raise stress; coolant rose from 2.98 % to 3.19 % | DECISION (manufacturability) |
| ED-046 | Linear stacking lean | Cancels design-point gas bending: hub bending stress 102 → about 0 MPa, peak hub fibre stress 273 → 171 MPa | CALCULATED (model output, not published) |
| ED-049 | Structural screening set-up: passage network kept, holes, ribs, pins and cut-back removed; tetrahedral quadratic mesh; centrifugal plus gas loads; a lofted 1.5 mm root fillet | Without the fillet the airfoil-platform junction was a re-entrant stress singularity; the local effects of removed features are noted, not computed | DECISION; results in [`../analysis_context/`](../analysis_context/README.md) |
| ED-050 | Flared shank, closed root-turn shape, 0.5 mm shank margin; life basis of at least 300 h rupture at the design point | Puts the airfoil walls over the shank (earlier-geometry screening FEA, box shank to flared shank: median von Mises 231 → 188 MPa in the 254–258 mm band, hub-band peak 942 → 593 MPa); the reference blade's life table shows about 250 h at maximum take-off consuming its whole life, so a design-point screen of 18,000 h would be the wrong test | DECISION; SOURCED + ASSUMED |
| ED-051 | Keep the serpentine direction; creep-matched cooling tried and not adopted | Reversing circuit B would cool the hub mid-chord wall about 100 K (model estimate, not published) but starve the trailing edge; the reference engine makes the same choice. Remaining risk: local creep strain and fatigue on the hub mid-chord wall | DECISION; open risk stated |
| ED-055 | Trailing-edge exit as an explicit cut-back channel; slot height 0.40 mm | With 1.0 mm walls the pin cavity pinched to zero height before the cut-back, so the slot was not connected in the CAD; 0.28 mm is below a plausible ceramic-core thickness. Same metering area as the 0.28 mm slot at 65 % open (0.28 × 0.65 = 0.40 × 0.455 mm) | DECISION; 0.40 mm ASSUMED |
| ED-056 | Film-row inlets checked against their supply cavity; suction-side gill row moved to 6.0 mm | An early layout had holes whose inlets lay in solid metal, which a volume comparison alone did not reveal; with the row at 6.0 mm all 12 holes are feasible | DECISION |
| ED-057, 058 | Corrections to an earlier model: trailing-edge core fillet reduced so the 0.40 mm channel survives; a straight neck added to the flared shank where it pinched at the B3 feed; hole overshoot raised to 0.6 mm for a 45° hole; trailing-edge exit modelled as 11 separate bleed-slot ducts and confirmed on the exported solid | The earlier model had a blind trailing-edge exit, a feed slot 0.0 mm from the shank surface and one suction-side hole not through; the exit is now confirmed by point classification on the final solid | DECISION |
| (hole set) | Drop three hole features (94 → 91) | See [`README.md`](README.md#from-94-to-91-hole-features) | DECISION; geometric reasoning CALCULATED, resulting solid measured |
| (rib surfaces) | Smooth rib surfaces with spline sides | Ribs modelled as 80-vertex polygons gave about 80 faces each; smooth sides give 6 faces per rib and cut the whole model from 3,846 to 1,542 faces with the same holes, pins, slots and passages | DECISION; VERIFIED |
